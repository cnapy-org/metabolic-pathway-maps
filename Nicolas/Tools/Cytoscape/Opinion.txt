"Cytoscape supports a variety of automated network layout algorithms, 
including spring-embedded layout, hierarchical layout, and circular layout (...) 
It models a mechanical system in which edges of the graph correspond to springs, 
creating an attractive force between nodes that are far apart, 
and a repulsive force between nodes that are close together."

"Whereas layout determines the location of the nodes and edges
in the window, an attribute-to-visual mapping allows data at-
tributes to control the appearance of their associated nodes and
edges. Cytoscape supports a wide variety of visual properties,
such as node color, shape, and size; node border color and thick-
ness; and edge color, thickness, and style; a data attribute is
mapped to a visual property using either a lookup table or inter-
polation, depending on whether the attribute is discrete valued
or continuous."
