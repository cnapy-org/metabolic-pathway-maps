 Internally, the data structure can be decomposed into three interconnected layers (Fig. 1). The last layer, or geometry layer, can display graphs as diagrams, according to a specific rendering style and local layout constraints. ... However the middle layer, or topology layer, is specific to Arcadia. At this level, the topology of the graph representation of the model can be modified without altering the model itself.
